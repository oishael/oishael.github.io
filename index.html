Illustrative work samples

How I approach a support issue.

These examples demonstrate my approach. They are fictional scenarios, not actual customer records or claims of work performed for Sift.

01 / Investigating a declined payment

Situation: A merchant reports that a customer’s card payment was declined.

Approach: Follow approved authentication, locate the transaction, and review the exact decline code and response. Determine whether one transaction or a broader set is affected. Treat the code as a starting point, check relevant account information, and follow the documented next step.

Customer communication: “I’ll review the transaction details and the response we received. That will help me explain the available next steps and determine whether another team needs to take a closer look.”

Escalation: Include the permitted transaction reference, timestamp, exact response, scope, customer impact, and actions already taken. Keep sensitive payment data out of unapproved channels.

02 / A technical escalation another teammate can use

Issue: A user can sign in but cannot complete a workflow.

Case structure: Customer goal → expected behavior → actual behavior → time and scope → environment → reproduction steps → troubleshooting performed → results → business impact → next owner and follow-up.

Decision: Use approved documentation to resolve known issues. Escalate when the issue remains outside my scope, with enough context to avoid restarting the investigation.

03 / Using AI thoughtfully in support

I have used tools including Virtual Assistant, ChatGPT, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot. AI can help organize information or draft a clearer explanation, but I verify the answer against approved procedures before relying on it.

My approach is to avoid entering confidential customer information into unapproved tools, check suggested steps for accuracy, and keep responsibility for the final customer response.
